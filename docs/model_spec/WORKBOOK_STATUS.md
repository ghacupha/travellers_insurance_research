# Formula-linked operating workbook

Run `scripts/build_operating_workbook.mjs` after rebuilding the historical, statement-control, operating and historical-integration JSON. It writes `output/travelers_operating_model.xlsx`. The renderer needs Node.js and `@oai/artifact-tool`; in this Codex workspace, the bundled runtime's `node_modules` is symlinked at the repository root and ignored by Git. The delivered workbook is generated by this project; a verified example release is checked in under `examples/travelers/deliverables/`. `rendered_row_map.json` is the **actual** row/period map for this renderer; `workbook_contract.json` remains the broader planning metric catalog.

The workbook has the specified Cover, Summary, Assumptions, Scenarios, Model, Output and Sources tabs. G holds 2019 opening balances, H:M hold audited 2020–2025 actuals, and N:R contain formulas for an **illustrative** 2026–2030 consolidated P&C premium and claims-reserve projection. Historical Model inputs link to individual Sources ledger facts rather than embedding numbers. Historical subtotals, gaps, average invested assets and yields calculate in cells. Source rows carry PDF URL/page/hash, SEC filing date and filing URL. The full historical balance-sheet section has zero asset, liability, equity-component and accounting-equation differences in all seven years. Historical GAAP income has zero revenue, expense, net-income and cash-flow net-income differences in all six flow years. The visible Summary Master Check reads **OPEN: historical stock bridges** while unresolved balances remain.

`Scenarios!D6` is the one live Base/Upside/Downside selector. Changing it changes the same forecast formulas through active Assumptions rows; historical cells remain fixed. `operating_scenarios.json` supplies the editable scenario assumptions, and `scripts/build_operating_forecast_reference.py` calculates the Python authority from the sourced 2025 opening state. The renderer compares nine forecast outputs across five years and all three cases (135 values) with that authority, then restores Base before export. The cases hold the FY2025 operating perimeter constant and **exclude the January 2, 2026 Canadian disposal**. They are analyst illustrations, not Travelers guidance or calibrated market forecasts. The workbook does not contain an integrated forecast income statement, balance sheet, cash-flow statement, segment projection, capital constraint, valuation or price target. The Output tab states that status. Historical earning, DAC, investment and debt differences remain visible on Model and are not fed forward as balancing entries. See [historical integration](HISTORICAL_INTEGRATION.md) for the transaction boundary.

The workbook was recalculated by the authoring engine, its core formulas and base/downside behavior were inspected, and all seven sheet previews plus the income/balance sections were reviewed. A native Excel or LibreOffice recalculation has **not** been performed because no such engine was available in the execution environment. Formula export was checked from the saved XLSX package; native-app formula and print behavior remain a validation gate.
