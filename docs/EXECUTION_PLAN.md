# Execution plan — FMI-style P&C insurance model

Date: 2026-09-23. Governing scope: the user's inherited FMI-style modelling
specifications, adapted to P&C insurance. This is a project specification, not a claim
of FMI certification. This plan supersedes the earlier three-year history and optional
Excel-renderer assumptions in the initial refactor roadmap.

## Deliverable and period policy

The principal deliverable is a reproducible, formula-linked Excel financial model and
supporting equity research output, generated by a reusable Python harness. JSON is the
auditable calculation/data interface, not a substitute for the financial workbook.

- Actuals: **2020–2025 inclusive (six fiscal years)**, matching the user's stated dates.
  Keep the window configurable; 2021–2025 would be the five-year alternative.
- Opening balances: **31 December 2019**, used for 2020 rollforwards and average-balance
  ratios. These are supporting inputs, not an extra forecast or disguised actual year.
- Initial annual forecasts: **2026–2030**, preserving the inherited five-year horizon.
- First release: FY2025-anchored model. Retain the existing **2026-02-12 information
  cutoff** provisionally. A current-date model is a separate run, adding available
  2026 interim results and current market inputs; do not silently mix the two views.
- The January 2, 2026 Canadian disposal changes the FY2026 operating perimeter.
  Normalize the sold business out of the opening operating schedules and account
  separately for sale consideration, derecognized assets/liabilities, gain/loss and
  reinvestment/repurchases before calling a forecast Travelers-specific. Until that
  bridge is sourced, the 2026–2030 workbook scenarios remain constant-perimeter
  illustrations and no integrated forecast or price target is approved.
- Fiscal periods, valuation date and information cutoff are separate configuration
  fields. A year-end 2025 balance is not evidence that it was public on 2025-12-31.
- Six annual observations help assess trends but are not enough by themselves to
  calibrate a robust catastrophe distribution, return regression or reserve model.
  Longer development histories and suitable quarterly/monthly data are separate inputs.

## Availability assessment

The [Travelers annual-report archive](https://investor.travelers.com/financial-information/annual-reports/default.aspx)
was checked on 2026-09-23. It lists PDF and HTML reports for each of 2020, 2021, 2022,
2023, 2024 and 2025, and 2019. The linked
[2020 report](https://s26.q4cdn.com/410417801/files/doc_financials/oar/v1/index.html)
and [2025 report](https://s26.q4cdn.com/410417801/files/doc_financials/oar/travelersAR2025V3/index.html)
were opened. Availability of the reports is confirmed; completeness and comparability
of every desired schedule row for every year are **not yet certified**.

Use SEC 10-K statements/notes as primary audited sources, issuer annual reports as
accessible copies, and earnings releases/statistical supplements for operating detail.
Discover supplement links during intake; the dynamic quarterly-results archive did
not expose a complete historical inventory in the text reader. SEC Company Facts is
a starting point for standard consolidated tags, not a complete insurance dataset.

For the FY2025 cutoff, use the underlying filing's actual dissemination date. An annual
report website published later does not itself establish point-in-time availability.
Maintain original filings and later restated/reclassified versions separately; select
the latest comparable facts available by the configured cutoff and explain changes.

Likely coverage: audited statements, premium totals, claims-reserve rollforwards,
investment notes, equity and share movements. Detailed treaty terms, segment-level
paid losses, every gross/ceded split and management operating drivers require a
year-by-year disclosure check. Keep unavailable detail explicitly missing; never
manufacture historical splits using forecast ratios.

## Phase 1 — Model specification and workbook contract

Inspect the workbook specification and existing renderer before choosing row maps.
The third-party teaching workbook is not distributed or required. Preserve the project conventions: schedules vertically organized on
one Model sheet, consistent periods, units, restrained formatting, print setup, clear
actual/forecast boundary, centralized assumptions and a visible Master Check.

Workbook tabs: Cover, Summary, Assumptions, Scenarios, Model, Output and Sources.
Historical disclosed inputs are hardcoded and sourced; historical subtotals and ratios
remain formulas. Forecasts are formula-linked. The inherited blue input / black formula /
teal cross-sheet / orange modeled-proxy conventions are project styling conventions.
Use one live scenario selector and clearly label multi-scenario snapshots.

**Acceptance:** a workbook row/period/source mapping and specification checklist that
maps each inherited requirement to its P&C replacement. No fixed three-year column
positions. Forecast year one links to the last actual column.

## Phase 2 — Historical source inventory and normalized facts

Acquire/cache 2020–2025 filings and the required 2019 opening data. Record URL,
accession/document identity, publication date, retrieval date and hash. Build a coverage
matrix: metric × year × segment × gross/net basis, with disclosed/derived/missing status.
Preserve currency/scale, instant versus duration, signs, period dates and table/tag locators.

Consolidated entity is The Travelers Companies, Inc. Segment views cover Business
Insurance, Bond & Specialty Insurance and Personal Insurance where disclosed; document
corporate/elimination bridges. Handle changes in classifications and held-for-sale items
explicitly. Reconcile rather than averaging incompatible sources.

**Acceptance:** six-year statements and required opening balances sourced; coverage
matrix shows all unresolved schedule details. No duplicate facts or silent zero fills.
Resolve or explicitly gate the existing FY2025 reported loss-ratio discrepancy against
filing/supplement evidence before declaring the ratio history fully reconciled.

## Phase 3 — Historical P&C schedules and reconciliations

Build in dependency order:

1. Direct/assumed GWP, ceded written premium, NWP; gross/ceded UPR and earned premiums/NEP.
2. Current accident-year attritional and catastrophe losses; signed prior-year reserve
   development; paid current/prior claims; gross/net loss and LAE reserves.
3. Reinsurance: unpaid recoverables, paid recoverables, prepaid premiums and collection
   movements. Do not use total recoverables as an unpaid-reserve offset.
4. Acquisition costs, DAC additions/amortization, operating expenses, commissions,
   policyholder dividends and issuer-specific fee allocations; reported versus underlying ratios.
5. Investment assets by disclosed class, carrying/fair-value basis, cash investments,
   purchases/disposals/maturities, net income/yield, realized gains/losses and OCI.
6. Debt/interest, current/deferred tax, equity/OCI, dividends, repurchases, issuance,
   period-end shares and diluted weighted-average shares; separate statutory capital.

**Acceptance:** premium earning, reserve, investment, DAC and equity rollforwards
reconcile within documented source-precision tolerances. FX/acquisitions/transfers
are explicit movements. Segment totals bridge to consolidated results. All ratios use
documented definitions and consistent numerator/denominator bases.

## Phase 4 — Driver-based forecasts and integrated statements

Project underwriting by segment: price/exposure/mix where supported, growth otherwise
explicitly modeled; reinsurance retention/earning; attritional loss trend, catastrophe
allowance, reserve development and claims payout; DAC/expense behavior. Project the
portfolio using opening holdings, maturities, reinvestment and funding cash flows.

Build net income only after these schedules. Integrate working capital, gross insurance
liabilities, separate reinsurance assets, investments, tax, debt and equity into balance
sheet and cash flow. Document cash/reinvestment timing to avoid hidden circularity.
Cash follows CFO/CFI/CFF plus relevant FX/reclassification bridges, not a balancing plug.
Link distributions to capital availability; distinguish GAAP equity from statutory surplus.

**Acceptance:** every historical and forecast period passes balance-sheet, cash,
reserve, premium, equity and investment checks. Stresses can expose funding/capital
shortfalls rather than allowing unlimited repurchases or unexplained negative balances.
Forecast assumptions are independent of historical disclosed facts.

## Phase 5 — Formula-linked Excel implementation

Develop the workbook skeleton early (Phase 1); populate verified historical schedules
as Phase 3 progresses, then add forecast formulas from Phase 4. Preserve reusable Excel
helpers and replace bank-specific layout/calculation modules. Avoid independent,
uncontrolled Python and Excel implementations: maintain a common row/formula contract
and test calculated workbook results against Python outputs.

The scenario switch must recalculate the integrated model. Static scenario comparisons
and Python-only simulation outputs are labelled as snapshots with run identifiers.
Changes to a workbook assumption affect live formulas; changing a Python simulation
assumption requires rerunning that simulation and refreshing its snapshot.

**Acceptance:** inspect formulas and references; recalculate in an available spreadsheet
engine and compare values for all three scenarios; inspect rendered/printed layout for
the full six-actual/five-forecast width. Writing XLSX formulas with openpyxl alone does
not count as recalculation. If no engine is available, report that validation limitation.

## Phase 6 — Valuation, scenarios and advanced analytics

Source dated price/share/market inputs. Implement dividend/distribution valuation with
explicit repurchase treatment, residual income with clean-surplus/OCI adjustments,
and insurer peer P/B/ROE and earnings multiples as cross-checks. Align all values to
one valuation date and discount future terminal comparables appropriately.

Add base/upside/downside operating cases and separate catastrophe, reserve, pricing,
reinvestment-rate and capital stresses. Regression needs a defensible sample, adjusted
returns or consistent peer metrics, diagnostics and sensitivity. Calibrated Monte Carlo
needs documented distributions/dependence; the current synthetic demo is not calibration.

**Acceptance:** reproducible scenarios, guarded terminal assumptions, transparent
valuation reconciliation and sensitivity. No unsupported automatic blend or recommendation.

## Phase 7 — Research output and reusable delivery

Generate the workbook, normalized/history/forecast JSON, source ledger, validation
report, run manifest and equity research report from the same approved result set.
Keep fetching, normalization, schedules, valuation and rendering independently runnable.
Document another US GAAP P&C issuer onboarding path without company-name conditionals
in the harness. Life and IFRS engines remain separate future work.

**Acceptance:** one documented script command rebuilds the model from pinned inputs;
replay produces matching numeric outputs; no unresolved material reconciliation is
presented as approved research. Remove obsolete bank code in a separate migration commit.

## Tests, delivery increments and immediate work

Unit tests cover identities, source/date/unit selection, restatements and invalid inputs.
BDD covers premium earning, incurred versus paid, reserve development, gross/net
reinsurance, source cutoffs, six-year actual windows, scenario propagation, capital
constraints and Python/Excel parity. Integration tests cover cache-to-workbook/report
runs, failed-stage artifacts and deterministic replay.

Use atomic commits: (1) workbook/data contracts, (2) source inventory/acquisition,
(3) normalized history by schedule group with reconciliations, (4) projection/statement
integration, (5) renderer/formula checks, (6) valuation/reporting. Include relevant tests
and docs in each commit. Do not commit generated run outputs or secrets.

The historical coverage matrix, consolidated premium/reserve actuals, audited
statement controls, operating cash/equity schedules and first formula-linked operating
workbook are documented in `docs/model_spec/`. Their passed checks do not imply a
completed integrated model. Continue remaining SEC row parity, investment/DAC/debt
stock bridges and segment detail, then integrated forecasts and valuation. No user
decision is required to begin those tasks.
Use issuer-hosted filings for public-source intake where practical; the SEC API adapter
needs the user's real contact identity before live requests. No contact is invented.
